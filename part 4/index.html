💌 Part 04 · The Love Notes

All the ways I love you.

Not just today, but every day, in every way.

❦
⭐ ADITI 16 DEC 2009 MY FOREVER
NOTE · 01 🌹

Every Day

“You’re gonna be my love forever.”

Har subah se leke har raat tak... chahe mausam kaisa bhi ho, my heart will always choose you, Aditi.

Chahe hum kitna bhi busy ho jayein, my day never feels complete without knowing you're smiling. Tu mera forever wala comfort zone hai.

NOTE · 02 ⏳

Every Moment

“I’ll love you every second, minute, hour, and day.”

Clock ki har ek tick ke saath, mera pyaar tere liye thoda aur gehra hota jaata hai. Ek pal bhi tere bina socha nahi jata.

60 seconds me 60 baar tera khayal aata hai. Time stops whenever you look at me with those cute expressive eyes.

NOTE · 03 ♾️

All My Life

“Every week, month, year, this life—and after rebirth.”

Sirf ye ek zindagi tere liye bohot chhoti hai... agle saat janmon me bhi sabse pehle main tujhe hi dhoondhunga, babyyygurlll.

Rebirth ke baad bhi mera pehla dialogue yahi hoga: "Arey Aditi, finally mil gayi! Maine pichle janam me bola tha na, tujhe kabhi nahi chhodunga."

NOTE · 04 🕊️

No Conditions

“I love you without expecting anything in return.”

Bina kisi shart ke, bina kisi expectation ke. Tera khush rehna hi meri sabse badi khushi hai, bas itna hi kaafi hai mere liye.

Pyaar me koi deal ya terms nahi hoti. You just be yourself—pagal, cute, moody—I love every single version of you unconditionally.

NOTE · 05 ✨

In My Prayers

“You’re the one person I pray for every single day.”

Bhagwan se apne liye mangne se pehle, meri har dua me tera naam, teri salamati aur teri smile hoti hai.

Mera favourite habit ye hai ki raat ko sone se pehle ek chhoti si prayer bolna: "Kanha ji, meri Aditi ko hamesha safe aur khush rakhna."

NOTE · 06 🌸

My Favorite Sight

“Seeing your smiling face makes my whole world bright.”

Puri duniya me sabse beautiful view tera haste hue chehra hai. Jab tu hasti hai na, sach me lagta hai sab theek ho jayega.

Vo dimple wali smile, vo innocent eyes... meri favourite photo gallery tera hasti hui shakal hi hai. Keep smiling always, my star.

💝
SPECIAL KEEPSAKE · FOR ADITI

A Promise Kept in My Heart

“Aditi, ye sirf likhe hue words nahi hain... ye mera dil hai jo sirf tere liye dhadakta hai. 16 December 2009 ko duniya me aane ke liye thank you. You made my life worth living, my birthday girl.”

Forever & Always, yours spidy 🕸️❤️
