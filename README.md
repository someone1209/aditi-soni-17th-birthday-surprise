# A Birthday Journey for Aditi Soni ✨🎂
*Dedicated to Aditi Soni (Born 16 Dec 2009 · Sweet 17) — crafted with infinite love by yours spidy 🕸️❤️*

Welcome to Aditi's 17th birthday interactive romantic website. The project is organized into 10 self-contained, beautifully animated chapters that flow seamlessly from one to the next.

---

## 📖 The 10-Chapter Story Flow
1. **[Part 01: The Human Check](part%201/index.html)** — Playful Hinglish identity quiz with dodging buttons.
2. **[Part 02: Cupid's Bow & Balloon Pop](part%202/index.html)** — Interactive archery aiming to pop 5 floating heart balloons.
3. **[Part 03: 3D Birthday Cake & Wish](part%203/index.html)** — Realistic 3D chocolate cake with 17 glowing candles and sparklers.
4. **[Part 04: The Love Notes](part%204/index.html)** — 6 love promise cards with 12 background themes and 3D tilt.
5. **[Part 05: Blooming Flower Garden](part%205/index.html)** — 16 botanical flowers that bloom on tap with kalimba melodies.
6. **[Part 06: Heart Clues Treasure Hunt](part%206/index.html)** — 12 memory trivia riddles to crack the `FOREVER ADITI` secret vault.
7. **[Part 07: Polaroid Memory Wall](part%207/index.html)** — 8 hanging polaroid photos with heartfelt handwriting notes.
8. **[Part 08: Scratch-Off Love Coupons](part%208/index.html)** — 3 metallic foil canvas scratch cards with secret rewards.
9. **[Part 09: Open When… Letters](part%209/index.html)** — 6 comforting handcrafted envelopes for every mood.
10. **[Part 10: A Birthday Love Letter](part%2010/index.html)** — 5-page 3D spiral notebook journal with realistic ruled paper.
